# AI-Powered Solutions for Healthcare Navigation

I am an international graduate student at Johns Hopkins University pursuing a Master's in AI in Business, and I also work as a research assistant. My academic and professional background has given me a strong interest in how emerging technologies can be applied to solve real-world challenges, particularly in domains where clear communication and accessibility matter most.

One problem I have observed is that navigating the healthcare system can be overwhelming and confusing, especially for international students who face language barriers and unfamiliar medical processes. From understanding insurance coverage to following up on a diagnosis, the complexity of the system can lead to missed care, poor health outcomes, and unnecessary stress for those who are already adjusting to a new environment.

Generative AI has the potential to make healthcare more accessible and understandable for everyone. Here are three ways it could help:

- **AI-powered visit summarization:** An AI assistant that listens to doctor visits and summarizes the key next steps for the patient in a simple, clear language — including medications, follow-up appointments, and lifestyle recommendations — so patients leave with a clear understanding of their care plan.
- **Multilingual health guidance:** A generative AI tool that translates medical documents, insurance explanations, and provider instructions into a patient's native language, reducing miscommunication and helping international students make informed decisions about their health.
- **Personalized healthcare navigation support:** A step-by-step guide for navigating a doctor’s visit from start to finish. This AI manages the logistics of the U.S. medical system: it verifies in-network providers, explains your 'Explanation of Benefits' (EOB), and provides a clear roadmap for care so you’re never navigating the system alone.
